# Taste

- Dark default. Linear-like density with slightly more breathing room in
  family mode; chronicle pages may go editorial (wider measure, serif
  voice for story text).
- Hard blacklist (workspace-wide, Ethan): gradients, glassmorphism,
  rounded-everything. They read as AI slop.
- Spacing pass on every mockup round (Marcel rule): explicit px
  annotations, obsessive rhythm.
- 1px inset shadow (10% black/white) instead of borders on images
  (Nils rule). Scanned records and portraits get this treatment.
- Evidence grades get quiet, consistent chips (A/B/C plus a distinct
  "myth" treatment), never loud badges in family mode.
- The four grandparent lines get four stable colors used identically in
  tree, map, and person chips. Color encodes lineage, nothing else.
- Map film: restrained basemap, muted terrain, lineage-colored routes;
  no cartoon markers. The bar is documentary title-sequence, not
  dashboard widget.
- Story voice (Ethan's pick, 2026-08-16, "B is probably best"): stories
  narrate THROUGH the reader's line: your ancestor anchors the telling
  and every other character is introduced by their relation to that
  ancestor, never cold. Evidence rides as numbered inline citation links
  resolving to an evidence section at the bottom of the page, and every
  story ends with a simple cast ledger table (person, lived, relation to
  you). Rejected: cast-first ledger openings (the ledger moved to the
  bottom), free-floating characters with no stated relation.
- AI imagery honesty (Ethan, 2026-08-16): AI-generated or AI-enhanced
  images are welcome in galleries ONLY with the honesty kit attached:
  kind badge ("AI reconstruction" / "AI-enhanced original"), method,
  disclaimer, and the documented evidence the image was built from.
  Real images always rank ahead of reconstructions in a strip.
- Reconstruction disclosure belongs in the interface, never in the image
  pixels (Ethan correction, 2026-08-22). Do not burn titles, captions,
  evidence grades, subtitles, badges, or provenance bands into generated or
  enhanced images. The gallery/lightbox supplies that context beside the clean
  image so the asset remains visually usable everywhere.
